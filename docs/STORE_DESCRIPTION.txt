🎉 PARTY PANIC 🎉
Grab your friends. Vote for the chaos. Survive the madness.

Every round is a different mini-game, and YOU pick it. Jump on a portal to vote, then dive into pure panic. Then do it again. And again.

🔨 DON'T GET FLATTENED: boulders, hammers and anvils rain down. Dodge or go pancake.
🌋 FLOOR IS LAVA: the floor is melting. Keep moving!
🥊 YEET ARENA: punch everyone off a shrinking platform with your Yeet Glove.
🛒 SHOPPING CART GRAND PRIX: race carts over ramps and boost pads to the finish.
💰 GREEDY GOBLINS: steal gold for your team. Bonk rivals to make them drop it.
🔘 THE WRONG BUTTON: find the right tile before the floor vanishes. Think fast!
