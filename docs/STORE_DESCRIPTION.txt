🎉 PARTY PANIC 🎉
Grab your friends. Vote for the chaos. Survive the madness.

🎈 PUMP & POP: pump your team's balloon until it pops!

🍪 CRAZY CUTTERS: trace the cookie. Most accurate cut wins!
