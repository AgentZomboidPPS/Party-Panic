🎉 PARTY PANIC 🎉
Grab your friends. Vote for the chaos. Survive the madness.

Every round is a different mini-game, and YOU pick it. Jump on a portal to vote, then dive into pure panic. Then do it again. And again. Up to 12 players, and friendly bots fill any empty spots so every round is packed.

🔥 FIRE ROPE: jump the swinging fire rope. It keeps speeding up!
🌋 HEX MELTDOWN: race to the hex that matches the flag before the rest sink into the lava.
🍯 HONEY TRAP: take 1 or 2 from the tree. Grab a honeycomb and the bees chase you out!
